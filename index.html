Selected work

Things I built in my current role.

01

Content calendar app

An approval workflow with Discord alerts for the social media team.

  • Each post moves through set steps: draft, copy approval, visual creation, revision, approved, scheduled and published.
  • Calendar and list views, with filters for status, platform and project.
  • Alerts in the app and on Discord, including when someone comments on a post.
  • An archive, analytics, a CSV export and a trash that empties itself after 30 days.

Built with Next.js and Supabase, hosted on Vercel

02

Hiring tracker

36 open roles with candidate stages, Excel export and Discord alerts.

  • Roles grouped by department and hiring wave, showing where each one is posted.
  • A candidate pipeline from initial screening to second round, final interview and offer.
  • An Excel export and a button that downloads every resume for HR.
  • Alerts in the app when a role or a candidate changes.

Built with Next.js and Supabase, hosted on Vercel

03

Bid notice tracker

Pulls government bid notices daily and alerts the bids team on Discord.

  • Sorts notices by category, such as energy, healthcare and manufacturing.
  • Each notice has one of five statuses: new, reviewing, pursuing, not pursuing or submitted.
  • Color coded deadlines show what is overdue, due soon or later.
  • A daily summary of open notices, grouped by category.

Built with Next.js and Supabase, hosted on Vercel

04

Team office page

One web page that shows the team, the work board, the post board and the meeting room.

  • An approvals box and calendar for content that is waiting for a decision.
  • Boards for social posts, articles, press releases and videos.
  • A voice meeting room for talking with the whole team.
  • A set of checks runs after every change.

Built with Python, HTML and JavaScript
